# Expert consensus, projections & market signals

## SUMMARY
## Expert consensus, projections and market signals: what a solo dev can actually use

**Main finding:** A public Play Store app can't legally get "consensus from all locations" for free. Almost every expert source is either paywalled, limited to personal or non-commercial use, or forbids scraping. What we can build, legally and for a long time, is a **calculated consensus** made from three things:

1. **Licensed or openly licensed data.** nflverse (CC-BY 4.0), the Fantasy Football Calculator ADP API (commercial use allowed) and FantasyCalc's public API.
2. **Betting-market signals**, which we pay for. The Odds API's paid tier is about $30–59/mo.
3. **Our own models**: usage, expected fantasy points (xFP), implied team totals.

Expert ECR (FantasyPros) should come in through a **paid Commercial licence**, or not at all in the public build.

### Decisions to make
1. **Sleeper's own docs say the API is free for *non-commercial* use; commercial use needs a licence.** This affects the whole product, not just projections. Ship the MVP free, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and contact Sleeper before monetizing.
2. **FantasyPros is the only real ECR-with-accuracy source.** Accuracy is scored in Half PPR. Experts' ranks are snapshotted at kickoff. Each rank slot gets a point value, the error is the gap between the predicted slot and the actual finish, and each expert's worst week is dropped. The free and HOF keys can't legally power a public app. Ask partners@fantasypros.com for a Commercial quote early. Expect a possible refusal under the "competes" clause, and design so ECR is an optional input that can be removed.
3. **Use Vegas as the core of the consensus.** Implied team total for the home team = total_line/2 + spread_line/2. nflverse already provides `spread_line` (positive = home team favored) and `total_line` under CC-BY. Player props (`player_reception_yds`, `player_rush_yds`, `player_anytime_td`, …) cost about 1 credit per market per region per event. Polling 16 games × 6 markets × 1 region a few times a week is about 2–3K credits a month, so the $30 tier is enough. Props are the most efficient and legally clean outside "expert" signal available.
4. **Architecture.** Use a GitHub Actions cron as the backend. It fetches keyed and non-CORS sources, computes *derived* numbers, and publishes JSON to Pages. Only republish raw data we have the rights to (nflverse, FFC, our own model outputs). Third-party data the user is personally entitled to (their Sleeper league) is fetched on the user's device.
5. **Build our own accuracy ledger.** Score each input stream (Sleeper/RotoWire, props-implied, our model, and FantasyPros if licensed) every week with FantasyPros' method, then weight the blend by measured accuracy. This is the legal way to say "we weigh sources by accuracy," and it sets the app apart.

### Not feasible
- Scraping 10+ expert sites for a public app.
- Using KTC values.
- Paywalled sites (4for4, Footballguys, ETR, FantasyPoints, PFF+) without a licence.
- Using X for free.
- Showing sportsbook links or affiliate ads on Play without gambling approval. Google cites an odds tracker with betting ads as an example violation.

### Net assessment
The report is broadly reliable on the facts. Endpoints, base URLs, headers, field names, file names, ID-map columns, the nflverse spread sign convention, the Google Play example and the overall architecture all hold up. Its strategic conclusion also holds: scraping expert sites is out, FantasyPros ECR needs a Commercial licence, and Vegas plus our own models should form the core. Where it errs, it errs in one direction: it is too optimistic about licensing.

1. FantasyCalc is not openly licensed. Its terms are non-commercial only, with attribution, and commercial use needs written permission.
2. ffopportunity xFP is CC-BY-SA, not CC-BY, so ShareAlike may attach to published blends.
3. nflverse Vegas lines come from an unlicensed repo of unstated provenance, not from CC-BY data.
4. Sleeper's General Terms of Use are stricter than the API docs about third-party commercial products.

So the 'legally clean MVP' is clean only while it stays non-commercial. Monetizing needs permissions from Sleeper, FantasyCalc and RotoWire (Sleeper's projection provider), not only Sleeper.

Smaller corrections:
- FantasyPros Premium keys also come with MVP (from $5.99/mo), and the commercial contact is support@ or api@fantasypros.com, not partners@.
- The FP accuracy method is point-based with z-scored weeks, not slot-based.
- The DP ECR feed is a twice-daily website scrape that covers only PPR for flex positions.
- The Odds API credit estimate is off by 4× (288 credits a week, not 1.2K), which makes props cheaper than stated.

Not verified: the Fantasy Nerds price, FFC commercial permission, the Odds API display/redistribution ToS and FantasyCalc CORS.

Method caveat: the WebSearch budget was used up before this check began, and direct access to sports sites was blocked. Primary-source checks came from cloning the GitHub repos (nflverse, ffverse, DynastyProcess, nfldata) and inspecting release-asset headers. Vendor terms and prices come from third-party GitHub captures dated mid-to-late 2026, plus training knowledge. Treat those vendor items as medium confidence and confirm them on the official pages before committing money or a launch plan.

If they refuse or it's too expensive, ask Fantasy Nerds for commercial permission (about $400/yr base) as the licensed ECR substitute. — FantasyPros ECR plus accuracy data is the gold standard for 'expert consensus' and the only realistic route to it is a licence. Lead times are long, so start now.
- [v1] Add The Odds API ($30/mo 20K tier) to pull NFL player props (pass/rush/rec yards, receptions, anytime TD) 2–3 times a week. Turn the lines into implied player projections (median from O/U adjusted for juice; TD probability from American odds) and blend them in as a high-weight input. — Betting markets are the most efficient public consensus and legally licensable. They also give injury-aware, real-time adjustments that weekly expert ranks miss.
- [v1] Build an in-house Source Accuracy Ledger that scores every input stream weekly with FantasyPros' method (Half PPR, kickoff snapshots, rank-value error, drop worst week) and sets blend weights by trailing accuracy. — This is the legal way to claim accuracy-weighted consensus. It gives the app a unique selling point and works with whatever sources are licensed.
- [v1] Add news and injuries: nflverse official injury reports plus the Sleeper player DB (injury_status, practice_participation, depth_chart_order) as structured signals. Add headline-and-link only from ESPN's site API and RSS (RotoBaller, PFR), and a curated Bluesky beat-writer list. — Free and structured, and showing headlines with links keeps copyright risk low. Real-time X data is too expensive before revenue.
- [v2] Once there is revenue, look at licensing RotoWire (news and projections, likely Sleeper's upstream) or PFF projections, and the X API for breaking news. — These close the remaining gaps in freshness and expert breadth, but they cost quotes in the thousands per year.
- [avoid] Don't scrape KTC, CBS, NFL.com, Yahoo, numberFire/FanDuel, FFToday, FantasySharks or paywalled sites (4for4, Footballguys, ETR, FantasyPoints, PFF+) for the public app. Don't show sportsbook links or affiliate ads. — KTC explicitly forbids it, and the other sites' ToS forbid scraping or commercial reuse. Any of these could get the Play listing pulled or bring a C&D, and Google Play bans odds trackers with gambling ads.
- [later] Offer an optional 'bring your own key' FantasyPros HOF integration only after legal review, and only if the call can come from the user's device or a pass-through that doesn't store data. — It could legally show ECR to users who personally hold HOF keys, but CORS and the 'competing product' clause make it uncertain.

## RISKS
- [high] Sleeper API terms limit free use to non-commercial purposes. Monetizing the app (ads, subscription, paid Play listing) without a Sleeper licence could get the app blocked or bring a C&D, and that would break league import, the core feature. — mitigation: Launch non-commercial. Contact Sleeper for licensing before monetizing. Plan ESPN, Yahoo (OAuth, also non-commercial) and MFL import as diversification.
- [high] FantasyPros refuses a Commercial licence or prices it out of reach because the app 'directly competes' with its Start/Sit, Trade and Waiver assistants, leaving no licensed source of expert ECR. — mitigation: Design the consensus to be source-agnostic, with ECR as an optional input. Fall back to Fantasy Nerds (with commercial permission) and Vegas props plus our own model. Market the 'calculated consensus' as the product.
- [high] Undocumented endpoints (Sleeper /projections, ESPN kona_player_info, ESPN site API) change or break mid-season. Sleeper's /v1/projections path already returned empty entries in 2026. — mitigation: Abstract each source behind an adapter with schema validation and health checks in Actions. Cache the last good snapshot. Keep at least two independent projection inputs (Sleeper plus props-implied) so one failure degrades gracefully.
- [medium] Republishing third-party data (DP's copy of FantasyPros ECR, Sleeper/RotoWire projections, FantasyCalc values) as static JSON on GitHub Pages counts as public redistribution, even if the app is free. — mitigation: Only publish CC-BY or explicitly permitted data and our own derived outputs. Fetch user-entitled data on the device. Get written permission from FantasyCalc. Keep an attribution and licence manifest per source.
- [medium] Play Store rejection or a policy strike for gambling-adjacent content if odds or props appear with sportsbook branding, links or ads. — mitigation: Show only derived metrics (implied totals or projections), with no bookmaker logos, deep links or affiliate offers. Fill in the content rating honestly. Add a short 'for informational purposes' note.
- [low] Odds API credit costs rise fast with props: 1 credit per market per region per event, and 10× for historical data needed for backtesting. — mitigation: Poll on a schedule (Tue opener, Thu, Sun AM), use one region and the 6 key markets, and track x-requests-remaining. Build the historical backtest from snapshots we take ourselves instead of paying for historical data.
- [medium] Pricing and terms in this research come partly from third-party reviews (Odds API tiers, Fantasy Nerds commercial tier, X API) and may be stale or wrong. — mitigation: Check each vendor's official pricing and ToS page before committing. Budget with headroom (+50%).
- [medium] nflverse injury data schema changed in 2025 (date_modified missing; 2025 rows reported missing), and ID mapping types differ between tables, so joins can silently fail. — mitigation: Parse defensively, cast IDs to string everywhere, cross-validate injuries against Sleeper, and alert from Actions when row counts drop sharply week over week.
- [low] GitHub Actions used as a data backend could breach GitHub's acceptable-use terms if it looks like a free compute farm, or hit minute limits on private repos. — mitigation: Keep jobs small (minutes per day). Use a public repo (unlimited minutes) or move to a Cloudflare Worker with Cron Triggers if the load grows.
